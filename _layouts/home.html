{%- comment -%} Thrum project section {%- endcomment -%}

side project

Thrum: Find a movie, together

What should we watch tonight? Thrum is a movie-matching app for couples, families, and friends. Choose your streaming platforms, set your mood, swipe together, and Thrum tells you the moment everyone likes the same thing. The app can also be used solo. I built it with Claude Code, directing the design and product decisions while it wrote the code.
