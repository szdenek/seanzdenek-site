Thrum

Find a movie,
together.

What should we watch tonight? Thrum is a movie-matching app for couples, families, and friends. Choose your streaming platforms, set your mood, swipe together, and Thrum tells you the moment everyone likes the same thing. The app can also be used solo.

Your People

Partner. Family. Solo. Your call.

Pair up with a partner, build a family group, or swipe solo. Every group is completely independent, so switching from movie night with your partner to family night never mixes up your matches.

How it works

Four taps to something you'll both actually watch

After you and your friend install the app, one of you simply has to create a group in the app and send the other person the six-digit code. That's it. Start swiping on your own devices. Swipe right to like and left to pass. When you both like the same movie or TV show, the app will notify everyone in the group. A group can be 2-12 people.

Features

Made for real nights, not ideal ones

📺 Only what you can actually watch

Pick your real streaming services and Thrum filters everything to just those streaming services you have access to. No subscriptions? One tap selects the free ones.

🎭 Set the mood in one tap

Quick-pick vibes for exactly the night you're having, a color wheel for painting your own, or filters by genre, decade, and origin.

🙋 Works solo, too

Waiting for a partner or friend to install the app? No problem: Swipe by yourself and build your own like, pass, and watched history. Everything works the same, with the exception of the matching mechanics.

🎬 Can't agree? Decide Right Now

If you still can't decide even after you've made multiple matches together, let the app pick something from your matches at random.

Quick picks

A mood for every night

Tap a quick pick and the filters fill in for you. Or paint your own mood: drag one or two dots around a color wheel, then fine-tune by genre, decade, and origin. Seasonal picks like Halloween and Foodie Night switch on by themselves, and partners can share moods with a tap too.

Real talk

Manage your matches

Every match lands on your Matches page, where you can leave notes and react with emojis. Pin up to five favorites to a shared shortlist, then pick a title: vote together, or let the app choose from your shortlist or from everything you've matched. After you've watched something together, move it to "watched together" and say whether it was worth it.

Decide Right Now

Can't agree? Let the app pick.

Tap Pick a title and let the app choose from your shortlist or from all your matches, things you've both already liked. The winner lands right at the top.

Taste

The data behind your taste, finally.

The Taste tab maps what you like by genre, and shows how your own taste shifts depending on who you're swiping with. Compare your solo likes with your likes in each group. Only you can see it. As you swipe and match, new match insights, shareable as a card, are delivered to your app weekly.

"What should we watch?" used to be the whole evening. Now it's a swipe.
